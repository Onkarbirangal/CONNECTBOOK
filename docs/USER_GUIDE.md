# ConnectBook End-User Guide

Welcome to ConnectBook! Connect with Friends. Share Your World.

---

## 1. Getting Started
- **Registration:** Visit `/accounts/register/` to sign up with your email, username, and name.
- **Login:** Sign in at `/accounts/login/` using your username or registered email.
- **Dark Mode:** Click your profile avatar in the navigation bar and toggle "Dark Mode" anytime.

---

## 2. Navigating the News Feed
The feed features specialized view tabs:
- **For You:** Personalized blend of content ranked by interaction recency, friendships, and following signals.
- **Friends:** Updates exclusively from your confirmed friends.
- **Following:** Updates from accounts and pages you follow.
- **Trending:** Posts with the highest engagement across ConnectBook.
- **Latest:** Strict reverse-chronological order of latest posts.
- **My Drafts:** Posts saved as drafts for later publishing.
- **Archived:** Your archived posts preserved privately.

---

## 3. Creating & Managing Posts
- **Post Composer:** Click "What's on your mind?" to open the rich composer.
- **Attach Media:** Upload photos and videos (up to 10MB per image, 50MB per video).
- **Post Status:** Choose to **Publish Immediately**, **Save as Draft**, or **Schedule Post**.
- **Turn Off Comments:** Switch off "Allow comments on this post" to disable replies.
- **Editing:** Click the 3 dots on your post and select "Edit Post". Edit history is tracked.
- **Pin / Archive:** Pin important posts to the top of your timeline or archive them.

---

## 4. Real-Time Messenger
- **1-on-1 & Group Chats:** Access Messenger from the chat icon in the top navbar or `/chat/`.
- **Quoted Replies:** Click the reply arrow on any message to quote it in your response.
- **Reactions:** Add reactions (👍, ❤️, 😆, 😮, 😢, 😡) to messages.
- **Edit & Delete:** Edit or soft-delete your sent messages.
- **Mute & Archive:** Mute chat notifications or move inactive chats to the Archived folder.

---

## 5. Privacy, Security & Data Portability
- **Privacy Center (`/settings/privacy/`):** Control default audience, story visibility, tagging, and mention permissions. Manage Blocked, Restricted, and Muted accounts.
- **Security & Sessions (`/settings/security/`):** View all active device sessions and terminate unauthorized sessions in one click.
- **Your Information (`/settings/your-information/`):** Download your complete data archive in JSON or schedule account deletion with a 14-day cancellation safeguard.
